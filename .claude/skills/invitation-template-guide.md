# Invitation Template Guide

What every Utsavy invitation template should have. Use this list while you build a template, and check it again before you hand the template over.

- **Must have**: the template is not finished without it.
- **Optional**: nice to have; add it if it fits the design.

All templates are paid templates.

---

## 1. Use the guest's name

Each guest opens their own link, so the invitation should greet them by name.

**Must have:** the guest's name appears in:
- the **Welcome section** (the first section after the opening animation);
- the **RSVP section**;
- the **Wishing wall**;
- the **Footer / Thank-you section**.

**Optional:** the guest's name in the **opening animation**.

**Also:** when there is no guest name (for example, the host's own preview), show a friendly fallback such as "Dear Guest".

---

## 2. Wishing wall that works with many wishes

A popular wedding can get a hundred wishes. The section must not grow taller and taller.

**Must have:**
- Show only **4 to 6 wishes** on the screen at a time.
- A **"View all wishes"** button (or something that fits the design: a popup, a slider, pages) to see the rest.
- The section keeps the same size whether there are 2 wishes or 200.
- A nice empty state when there are no wishes yet.

---

## 3. Events section

**Must have:**
- Designs for **as many events as the template's tradition usually has** (for example Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception), each with its own image or illustration.
- A **general (global) event design**. A real user may add an event the template doesn't have; it must still look good with this design.
- For every event:
  - **Event name**
  - **Date**
  - **Time**
  - **Address**
  - **Dress code**
  - **View location** (opens the address in Maps)

**Optional:**
- **Save to calendar**: one button that saves **all the events together** in a single tap, not one button per event.

---

## 4. Gallery

**Must have:**
- Each item is **one photo and one caption**.
- Keep it clean and simple; don't crowd the section.
- Even **3 photos** is fine.

---

## 5. Contact

**Must have:**
- **One person**: their **name**, their **relation** (for example "Bride's brother"), and **one phone number**.
- The same number is used for both a **phone call** and **WhatsApp**.

---

## 6. Travel & stay

**Must have:**
- A **message for guests** (for example how to reach, where to stay).
- The **travel / stay name** (the hotel, venue or transport).

**Optional:**
- An **image or illustration** for the travel / stay.

---

## 7. Our story

The couple's story, told in chapters.

**Must have:** for each chapter:
- **Title**
- **Chapter number**
- **Time & place**

**Optional:**
- **Description**

---

## 8. RSVP

**Must have:**
- Keep it **simple: just "Accept invitation"**.
- After the guest accepts, play an **animation** that matches the template's theme (or something specific to the couple).
- A guest who has already accepted sees that they've accepted, not the button again.
- The guest's name is used here (see rule 1).

---

## 9. Blessings

**Must have:**
- A **message box** for the guest to send a blessing / wish.
- Greet the guest by name above it, for example "Dear Ravi, send your blessings".
- The blessings shown keep the section the same size, however many there are (same idea as rule 2: a few at a time, the rest behind a button or slider).

---

## 10. Footer / Thank-you section

**Must have:**
- **Wedding details** (date, place)
- **Couple details** (names)
- The **guest's name**
- A **thank-you message**

Anything more is up to the developer's creativity.

---

## 11. Countdown

**Must have:**
- A **countdown to the wedding** (days, hours, minutes), or a **"Save the date"** style section.

---

## Good practice (all templates)

- Design for **mobile first**; most guests open the link on a phone.
- If the host leaves a detail empty (no dress code, no gallery photos), **hide that part neatly** instead of showing a blank box.
- Keep images light so the invitation opens fast on mobile data.
